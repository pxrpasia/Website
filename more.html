KNOW THE CITY

SERVER RULES.

01 — ROLEPLAY FIRST

Stay in character and value the roleplay experience of everyone around you.

02 — RDM / VDM

Random deathmatch and vehicle deathmatch are not allowed without valid roleplay context.

03 — FEAR RP

React realistically when your character is threatened with serious harm.

04 — METAGAMING

Do not use information your character could not reasonably know in roleplay.

05 — POWERGAMING

Do not force unrealistic actions or outcomes onto another player's character.

06 — COMBAT LOGGING

Do not disconnect to avoid an active roleplay situation, arrest or consequence.
