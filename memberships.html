PRIVACY & POLICIES.

📌 01 — GENERAL POLICY

By purchasing any item, package, or service from the Prime X Roleplay store, you agree to follow all server rules and these shop policies.

💳 02 — PAYMENTS & TRANSACTIONS

All payments are final and non-refundable unless stated otherwise.

Make sure to double-check your purchase before completing payment.

Payments are processed securely through our store provider.

Chargebacks or disputes will result in:

All items are virtual goods (cars, roles, perks, etc.).

Delivery is usually instant, but may take up to 24 hours.

If you do not receive your purchase, contact support with proof of payment.

⚠️ 04 — NO REFUND POLICY

We do NOT offer refunds for:
• Change of mind.
• Server wipes or updates.

Refunds are only considered in rare cases, such as a duplicate purchase or technical error.

Any attempt to exploit, duplicate, or abuse store items will lead to:

• Permanent server ban.

Purchased perks must not be used to gain unfair advantage or break RP rules.

🔄 06 — PACKAGE CHANGES

Server packages may be updated, modified, or removed at any time to maintain balance.

You will not lose access, but features may change for fairness.

👮 07 — STAFF RIGHTS

Admins have full rights to:
• Remove perks if abused.
• Adjust benefits for balance.
• Take action against rule-breaking players.

📞 08 — SUPPORT

If you face any issue, contact us via a support ticket.

Provide:

• Your in-game name.
• Description of the issue.

🧾 09 — FINAL AGREEMENT

By purchasing from Prime X Roleplay, you confirm that:
• You understand these policies.
• You agree to follow server rules.
• You accept the no-refund terms.
